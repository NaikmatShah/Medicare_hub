Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking, managing, and paying for appointments.

How do I book an appointment?

Search for a doctor by specialty, location, or consultation type on the Find Doctors page, open their profile, choose an available time slot, and confirm your details.

Can I book on behalf of a family member?

Yes. During checkout, enter the patient's details in the patient information section — you can manage multiple family members from a single account.

What's the difference between in-person and video consultations?

In-person visits take place at the doctor's clinic, while video consultations happen over a secure video call from your phone, tablet, or computer. Not every doctor offers both options.

Do I need an account to book an appointment?

Yes, a free account keeps your appointment history, medical notes, and upcoming visits organized in one dashboard.

How do I update my profile information?

Go to your patient dashboard and open Profile Settings to update your name, email, and contact details at any time.

Is booking through MediCare Hub free?

Yes — creating an account and booking appointments is always free. You only pay the consultation fee set by each doctor, shown upfront before you confirm.

When am I charged for a visit?

You're not charged when you book. Payment is processed after your visit is completed, using the payment method on file.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Open your dashboard, go to Appointments, and choose Reschedule or Cancel next to any upcoming visit — no phone call required.

What should I bring to an in-person visit?

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if applicable, and a list of any current medications. Arrive 10 minutes early for check-in.

What if I'm running late for a video consultation?

Doctors hold video slots for 10 minutes past the start time. If you're delayed further, message support and we'll help you rebook.
