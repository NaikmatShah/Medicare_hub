Frequently asked questions

Is booking through MediCare Hub free?

Yes — creating an account and booking appointments is always free. You only pay the consultation fee set by each doctor.

Can I book a video consultation instead of visiting in person?

Absolutely. Filter for "Video call" while searching, and you'll only see doctors offering remote consultations.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Open your patient dashboard, go to Appointments, and select Reschedule or Cancel — no phone call required.
